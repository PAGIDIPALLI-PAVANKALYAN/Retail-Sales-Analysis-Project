# Retail Sales Business Analysis – Key Insights

## 1. Project Objective

Analyze retail transaction data to understand sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product performance, payment preferences, and monthly sales trends.

## 2. Overall Business KPIs

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | ₹215,489,969.15 |
| Total Profit | ₹100,662,489.15 |
| Total Cost | ₹114,827,480.00 |
| Total Transactions | 124,754 |
| Total Units Sold | 268,592 |
| Average Transaction Value | ₹1,727.32 |
| Profit Margin | 46.71% |

## 3. Product Performance Insights

- Mixer Grinder generated the highest product revenue.
- Mixer Grinder revenue: ₹29,832,739.30.
- Mixer Grinder profit: ₹9,621,489.30.
- Product-level analysis helps identify high-performing products and opportunities for improvement.

## 4. Customer Segment Insights

Small Business was the leading customer segment.

| Segment | Revenue (₹) | Profit (₹) | Transactions |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Consumer | 71,704,739.30 | 33,476,754.30 | 41,635 |
| Corporate | 71,545,114.75 | 33,444,874.75 | 41,348 |
| Small Business | 72,240,115.10 | 33,740,860.10 | 41,771 |

Business finding: Revenue and transaction volumes are relatively balanced across the three customer segments.

## 5. Payment Method Insights

- Net Banking generated the highest revenue: ₹43,701,876.60.
- Net Banking generated the highest profit: ₹20,403,736.60.
- Cash had the lowest revenue: ₹42,364,905.45.
- All five payment methods contributed relatively balanced transaction volumes.

## 6. City Performance

City-level revenue, profit, and transaction analysis was performed using Excel Pivot Tables.

## 7. Monthly Sales Trends

Monthly sales performance was analyzed across 2024 and 2025 to compare revenue trends and identify changes in business performance.

## 8. Business Recommendations

1. Maintain inventory availability for high-performing products.
2. Develop targeted marketing strategies for customer segments.
3. Continue supporting all major payment methods.
4. Investigate city-level sales differences to identify growth opportunities.
5. Monitor monthly revenue and profit trends to support business planning.

## 9. Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning, Pivot Tables and Dashboard.
- Python – Pandas, NumPy and data analysis.
- MySQL – SQL queries and business analysis.
- Power BI – Interactive dashboards and KPI visualization.
- AI Assistance – Support for SQL generation, data validation, analysis and documentation.

## 10. Project Outcome

Developed an end-to-end retail sales analytics project that transforms transactional data into business insights using Excel, Python, MySQL and Power BI.
